Recant

Who did it?

Pick someone to see their story. You accuse them from there.

🔴 story changed  ·  🟢 honest mix-up  ·  ⚫ nothing in it

How to play

Six people were in the building when it happened. One of them is lying about where they were.

  1. Look for a room with two names in it. Two people can’t both have been alone in the same room — unless they were really in there together.
  2. Tap a name to ask who else was there. People who really were together will name each other. Asking is free.
  3. Challenge a room where the stories don’t fit. The liar will change their story to a new room. Follow them until they run out of rooms.
  4. Then accuse them. If you chased their story all the way, you’ve proved it. Accuse without that and you only guessed.

You have four challenges. Everyone here has something small they’d rather not talk about — only one of them is lying about where they were.
