Recant

The statements

How it works

Five people were on shift. One of them is lying about where they were. Nobody will tell you which.

Read

Everyone gives an account. Two people claiming the same room at the same time is a conflict — unless they were genuinely together, in which case they will say so.

Press

Put one account to another. Nothing is flagged for you; finding the conflict is the game. A liar recants and gives you a new story. An honest witness who simply misremembered will correct themselves, and that thread is closed.

Four

You get four pressings. Cornering the liar takes three, so you can afford to be wrong once.

Name

Name anyone, any time. If you pressed them until their story ran out, you have proved it. Name them without that and it counts, but you only guessed.

Everyone here has something small they would rather not discuss. Only one of them is lying about the thing that matters.
