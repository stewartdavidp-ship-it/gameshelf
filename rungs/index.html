👋 Welcome to Rungs!

Pair words into everyday phrases, then find the hidden ladder they climb.

Is this your first time playing?

Step 1: Find the phrases

JET SMALL PRINT TALK

You get 8 words. Tap two that make an everyday phrase, like SMALL + TALK, then tap Place. Tap ⇄ on a rung to flip its words.

Watch for traps: SMALL PRINT is a phrase too, but then TALK has no partner.

Step 2: Find the hidden ladder

4. JUMBO JET
3. LARGE PRINT
2. MEDIUM RARE
1. SMALL TALK

One word in each phrase is a step on a real-world ladder. Here it's sizes: small, medium, large, jumbo. The ladder word can be either word in its phrase.

📏 Stack it so the ladder climbs: lowest at the bottom, highest at the top. Use ▲ and ▼ to move a rung.

Step 3: Submit

You have 4 attempts.

If a pair is wrong, you'll see which ones. The words in wrong pairs belong with each other.

Harder days hide the ladder in trickier ways. Tap 💡 Hint to see how.

Once all four pairs are right, the ladder words light up and you can guess the ladder for free:

SMALL · MEDIUM · LARGE · JUMBO
What is it? Steak? Airplanes? Sizes?

Step 4: The ladder hides

SECOND HAND · MINUTE MAN · HOUR GLASS · DAY DREAM

Most days the ladder is better hidden. Those four climb units of time: second, minute, hour, day.

Look for the word in each phrase that means something else. That's the moment it clicks.

Try a practice puzzle now. It won't count.

How to Play

Goal: Pair the 8 words into 4 everyday phrases, then stack them so a hidden ladder climbs.

Step 1: Tap two words that make a phrase, then Place. Tap ⇄ to flip the words on a rung; the order inside a rung never counts against you

Step 2: One word in each phrase is a step on a real-world ladder: a score, a size, a scale. It can be either word in the phrase. Order the rungs with ▲ and ▼

Step 3: Tap Submit. You have 4 attempts

📏 Example: SECOND HAND, MINUTE MAN, HOUR GLASS, DAY DREAM climb units of time: second, minute, hour, day.

💡 Stuck? Harder days hide the ladder in trickier ways. Tap Hint to see how. Once all four pairs are right, you can also guess the ladder for free.
