❓ How to Play

Guess the author of the daily quote in 4 tries or less!

After each guess, you'll receive hints about:

  • ✓ Gender (if correct)
  • ✓ Category (Literature, Philosophy, etc.)
  • ✓ Time Period (Ancient to Modern)

💎 Hint Tokens: Earn tokens by winning! Spend on extra hints:

  • 🔤 First Letter - 1 token
  • 👥 Narrow to 5 authors - 2 tokens
⌨️ Keyboard Shortcuts
  • Enter - Submit guess
  • S - Open menu
  • P - Practice mode
  • A - Achievements
  • Space - Play/pause audio
  • Esc - Close modal
